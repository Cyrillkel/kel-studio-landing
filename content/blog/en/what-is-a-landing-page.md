A landing page is a one-page website that sells one thing and leads the visitor to one action: leave a request, call, book, buy. The name comes from the visitor "landing" on it after clicking an ad, a search result or a social media post.

The main difference from a regular website is that a landing page has nothing extra on it. No menu with twenty sections, no news, no blog. Everything on the page works toward one goal.

## How a landing page differs from a multi-page website

| | Landing page | Business card site | Corporate website |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pages | one, long | 3-7 | dozens |
| What it is about | one offer: a service, a product, a promotion | you and everything you do | the company, all its services and products |
| Goal | one action | introduce you and give contacts | tell about the company, attract clients from search |
| Where it works best | for advertising and for one search query | as the address for business cards and profiles | as the company's main site |
| Our price guideline | from $500 | from $200 | from $1,000 |

The same business can have both: a corporate website as a permanent address and several landing pages for different services and ad campaigns.

## When you need a landing page

- **You are launching a new product or service** and want to test demand quickly. One page takes 1-2 weeks, not a month.
- **You attract clients through advertising.** [Yandex Direct](/services/yandex-direct) brings a person to a specific page, and the more exactly it answers their query, the more often they leave a request. A general home page of a site copes with advertising worse.
- **You sell one clear service.** Renovation, training, a course, an event, a consultation.
- **You need a page for a promotion or an event** that will run its term and close.

## When a landing page is not enough

- **The company has several different lines of business.** If you fit them all on one page, it turns into a long catalog where it is hard to find what you need.
- **You need to attract visitors from search across many queries.** One page answers a limited range of queries. A separate page for each noticeable query works better, and that is already a [corporate website](/services/corporate).
- **You need a catalog, a personal account, payment on the site.** For such tasks you need an online store or a web application.
- **You need an address for your business card and profiles that tells about you as a whole.** A [business card site](/services/vizitka) fits here.

## What a good landing page consists of

The set of blocks depends on the product, but almost always it rests on one sequence, like a conversation with a client:

1. **The first screen.** What you offer, to whom and what the benefit is, plus a button. If a person has not understood this in a few seconds, they will not go further.
2. **Who it is for and what problem it solves.** A recognizable situation of the client, the one they came to you with.
3. **How it all works.** The stages of work or what the service includes. Specifics remove the fear of the unknown.
4. **Examples and proof.** Work, reviews, figures that can be checked. No invented numbers: people can feel made-up figures.
5. **The price, or what it depends on.** Many leave if they have not understood what it costs. If you cannot name an exact sum, explain what affects it.
6. **Answers to frequent questions.** Every objection you hear on the phone should be answered on the page.
7. **A request form or a contact button.** Ask only for what you cannot answer without: a name and a phone number or an email. Every extra field reduces the number of requests.

## What affects the result

- **Match between the ad and the page.** A person clicked an ad about one thing and sees something else on the page: they leave. The page headline should answer the query they came from.
- **Speed on a phone.** A noticeable share of visitors come from a phone, and a heavy page loses them before the first screen.
- **One call to action.** If the page has five different buttons with different actions, a person does none of them.
- **Connected analytics.** Without [Yandex Metrica](https://metrica.yandex.com/) you will not know where requests come from and how much each one costs. We connect Metrica in every landing page.

## What to prepare before you order

The work goes faster if you answer a few questions in advance:

- what exactly you sell and to whom;
- how you are better or more convenient than the alternatives, in a phrase or two;
- what action the visitor should take;
- what examples of work, reviews and photos you have;
- where the traffic will come from: advertising, search, social media, a mailing.

If you do not have the answers, that is fine: we work out the structure of the page together with you at the brief. Describe your task in a request, and we will suggest whether you need a landing page or something simpler is enough.
